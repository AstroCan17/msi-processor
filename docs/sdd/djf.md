# Design justification file

## Introduction

The Design justification file (DJF) regroups the documentation justifying the
evolution of the product from the requirements to the design and demonstrating that the design
conforms to the requirements and is complete and correct.

This document covers the:

- Requirements justification with a full traceability to the design, including their allocation
across the elements to ensure that all requirements are fully and correctly covered by the
collaboration between elements.
- High level trade-offs triggered by the design drivers.
- COTS justification for the ones that are key to the project.

The intended audience of this document is people involved in the analysis of the technical solution,
including the requirement analyses, performance analysis and architectural design.

### Requirements justification

This sections describes the reasons and justifications of the chosen implementation solutions for the requirements.

### Trade-offs

This section describes technical and configuration trade-offs with respect to technical performance, reliability, lifetime, and cost.

### COTS justification

This section summarizes the approach of the COTS selection and their justification.
